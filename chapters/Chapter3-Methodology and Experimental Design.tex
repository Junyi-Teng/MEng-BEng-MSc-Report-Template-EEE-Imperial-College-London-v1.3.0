\section{Methodological Overview}
The experimental framework contains four principal routes: \acro{AWQ} as
a weight-only reference, SmoothQuant as a joint weight--activation
reference, a project-specific \acro{W4A8} pipeline, and NestQuant as a
structured low-bit method that extends the quantization scope to model
weights, runtime activations and the key--value cache.
